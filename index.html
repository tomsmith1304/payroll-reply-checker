How the checks work

Compliance — flags any of 12 terms the customer raises that the reply never touches: certified payroll, prevailing wage, apprentice ratio/rate, overtime, union rules, per diem, fringe benefits, misclassification, 1099/W-2 status, FLSA, Davis-Bacon.

Escalation — a fixed list of payroll-blocking phrases (bounced checks, missed payday, "haven't been paid," failed direct deposits) trips the red banner regardless of what the reply says.

Tone — flags a reply that reads short relative to a long message, urgency or frustration with no acknowledgment before the fix, or ALL-CAPS text.

All keyword and pattern matching — no model call, nothing leaves the browser.
